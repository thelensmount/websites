Syncing between devices

ForwardLog keeps your logs in your own iCloud account and syncs them to every iPhone, iPad and Mac signed in to the same Apple Account. To sync, iCloud must be on for ForwardLog: on iPhone and iPad, go to Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ iCloud; on a Mac, go to System Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ iCloud. If your iCloud storage is full, ForwardLog shows a notice, and changes sync again once there's space.

Your data

Your logs are stored on your devices and in your private iCloud database. We can't see them. To keep a copy elsewhere, use the Share button on any page to copy or share one page or all pages as a checklist, or to save them as a Markdown file.

Due dates and moving to-dos

Dates you type, such as "tomorrow", "Friday" or "Dec 25", become a to-do's due date, and to-dos due tomorrow or sooner move to the top of Today. You can turn either of these off in Settings.

Undo

Use the Undo and Redo buttons on iPhone and iPad, or ⌘Z and ⇧⌘Z with a keyboard.
